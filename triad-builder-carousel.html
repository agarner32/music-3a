Triad Builder — Worked Examples

Six examples walking through the major scale method and adjustment rules.
The last example shows the enharmonic spelling trick for difficult roots.

The Building Rules
Reference

Every triad quality is built from a major triad. Start with scale degrees 1, 3, and 5 of the major scale, then make the adjustment below.

M
Major — 1, 3, 5 of the major scale. No adjustment needed.
m
Minor — Lower the 3rd by a half step.
d
Diminished — Lower both the 3rd and the 5th by a half step.
A
Augmented — Raise the 5th by a half step.
For roots that don't belong to a common major key, use the enharmonic spelling trick shown in the last panel.
E♭ major triad
Major
1
Start with the E♭ major scale. Scale degrees 1, 3, and 5 are E♭, G, B♭.
2
Major triad = 1, 3, 5 of the major scale. No adjustment needed.
3
The triad is complete as spelled. E♭ to G is a major 3rd. G to B♭ is a minor 3rd. E♭ to B♭ is a perfect 5th.
E♭ Major Triad
E♭ – G – B♭
A minor triad
Minor
1
Start with A major: scale degrees 1, 3, 5 are A, C♯, E.
2
Minor = lower the 3rd by a half step. C♯ lowered by a half step becomes C♮.
3
The 5th (E) stays the same. A to C♮ is now a minor 3rd. C♮ to E is a major 3rd. A to E is still a perfect 5th.
A Minor Triad
A – C – E
B diminished triad
Diminished
1
Start with B major: scale degrees 1, 3, 5 are B, D♯, F♯.
2
Diminished = lower both the 3rd and the 5th by a half step.
D♯ → D♮   |   F♯ → F♮
3
B to D♮ is a minor 3rd. D♮ to F♮ is a minor 3rd. B to F♮ is a diminished 5th — the tritone. Two minor thirds stacked.
B Diminished Triad
B – D – F
C augmented triad
Augmented
1
Start with C major: scale degrees 1, 3, 5 are C, E, G.
2
Augmented = raise the 5th by a half step. G raised by a half step becomes G♯.
3
The 3rd (E) stays the same. C to E is a major 3rd. E to G♯ is also a major 3rd. C to G♯ is an augmented 5th. Two major thirds stacked.
C Augmented Triad
C – E – G♯
D♯ major triad
Enharmonic Trick
1
D♯ is not a common major key root. Replace it with its enharmonic equivalent: D♯ = E♭.
2
Build E♭ major: scale degrees 1, 3, 5 are E♭, G, B♭.
3
Respell every note back to match the sharp spelling, keeping the same letter names:
E♭ → D♯   |   G → F𝄪   |   B♭ → A♯
4
The third must stay on an F (F𝄪, not G♮) and the fifth must stay on an A (A♯, not B♭). Letter names must be preserved — only the accidentals change.
D♯ Major Triad
D♯ – F𝄪 – A♯
